02. About

Modelling the Decisions
High-Tech Companies Face

I take a decision an engineering company is considering and build the financial model that supports it. In particular, I am interested in using data-driven insights to support business decision-making in the high-tech industry.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    Start from a real choice: is this investment, production ramp-up or sourcing option worth it?

  2. 02

    Build the model

    Capacity, cost and cash flow in Python or Excel, valued with NPV and tested across scenarios.

  3. 03

    Give the answer

    What the numbers support, what the result depends on, and where the assumptions are weakest.

In practice · Airbus A320 ramp-up Is raising production to 75 aircraft a month worth the investment? My model says yes, but only if suppliers can support at least about 82% of the plan. Read the case →
