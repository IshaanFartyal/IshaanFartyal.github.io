Factory Operations / Production Study
DAF Factory Analysis
01 Situation

Understanding factory performance

The project focuses on understanding how production flows through the factory and how operational constraints affect throughput, capacity and overall production performance.

02 Task

Identify operational constraints

The objective is to determine where capacity limitations and process bottlenecks occur, and assess how these influence production efficiency and achievable output.

03 Action

Analyse the production system

I analysed the factory as an integrated production system, examining process flow, station capacity and operational dependencies.

  • Mapped the production process and major operational stages.
  • Compared capacity across production steps.
  • Identified potential bottlenecks and limiting operations.
  • Evaluated the impact of production constraints on throughput.
  • Considered opportunities for operational improvement.
04 Result

Translate analysis into operational insight

The analysis provides a structured view of where production performance is constrained and which areas offer the greatest potential for improvement.

Decision output

Prioritize operational interventions based on their potential impact on capacity, production flow and overall factory performance.

05. Analytical Framework
Process Production flow

Comparing available capacity across production stages.

Operations Bottleneck analysis

Identifying the operations limiting throughput.

Improvement Operational priorities

Evaluating where changes could improve production performance.
