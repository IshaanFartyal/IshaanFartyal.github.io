01 / Context

Engineering with a business lens

At University Racing Eindhoven, I worked first as a Mechanical Design Engineer and later as a Cost Engineer. Together, the two roles gave me experience on both the technical design side and the financial and lifecycle implications of engineering choices.

02 / Design

Mechanical Design Engineer

I designed an in-wheel motor assembly in Siemens NX, translating requirements, preferences and constraints into a detailed CAD assembly.

  • Considered packaging and vehicle integration.
  • Evaluated manufacturability and production limitations.
  • Iterated the design with other vehicle sub-teams.
03 / Cost

Cost Engineer

I developed and maintained detailed Bills of Materials and Costed Carbonized Bills of Materials to evaluate component cost and lifecycle emissions across manufacturing and distribution.

I also built an Excel cost model for the inverter, comparing in-house manufacturing and assembly with outsourcing across different production volumes to identify break-even points and sourcing implications.

04 / Decision making

Turning engineering into business insight

Core learning Engineering choices can be evaluated not only on technical performance, but also on cost, scalability, sourcing, manufacturability and lifecycle impact.

This experience is what first pushed me toward problems at the intersection of engineering, operations and business strategy.
