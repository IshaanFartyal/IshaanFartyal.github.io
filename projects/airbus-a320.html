Airbus A320 Capacity Analysis

Evaluating alternative Airbus A320 production ramp-up scenarios by combining operational modelling with financial analysis to assess feasibility and profitability.

Python Capacity Analysis Supply Chain Scenario Analysis NPV
GitHub Repository ↗
Production System / Capacity Study
75 Aircraft / month target
01 Situation

Scaling A320 production

Airbus has announced plans to increase A320-family production toward 75 aircraft per month. Achieving this requires production capacity and supply-chain availability to scale alongside the planned ramp-up.

02 Task

Evaluate viable ramp-up paths

The objective is to compare alternative production-ramp scenarios and determine which pathways offer the strongest balance between operational feasibility and financial attractiveness.

03 Action

Build a quantitative scenario model

I developed a Python-based capacity model to compare alternative ramp-up trajectories, supply-chain availability and steady-state production targets.

  • Defined alternative production-ramp scenarios.
  • Modelled capacity and supply-chain availability over time.
  • Compared alternative steady-state production rates.
  • Calculated NPV for each production strategy.
  • Assessed operational and financial trade-offs across scenarios.
04 Result

Prioritize realistic strategic options

The model provides a structured basis for comparing production strategies across operational feasibility and financial performance.

Decision output

Identify which ramp-up pathways are operationally realistic, financially attractive and suitable for prioritization as a strategic course of action.

05. Analytical Framework
Operational Production capacity

Ramp trajectories and achievable monthly output.

Supply Chain Availability constraints

Ability of upstream capacity to support production growth.

Financial NPV

Financial attractiveness of alternative production scenarios.

Strategic Scenario prioritization

Balancing feasibility, return and ramp realism.
